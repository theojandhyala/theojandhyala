WHO IS THEO JANDHYALA?

Theo Jandhyala is an independent app developer based in the Cheltenham area, England. He is the creator of Cast, DEADSET, Reclaim and LifeScore. Cast and DEADSET are available on the Apple App Store; Reclaim and LifeScore are development portfolio projects in release preparation.

THE WORK BEHIND THE APPS

From first idea
to working product.

I’m Theo Jandhyala, an independent app developer based in the Cheltenham area. My work spans product planning, interface design, development, testing and release preparation.

My projects use Swift and SwiftUI for native iOS, alongside TypeScript, React, Next.js and Capacitor for web and mobile experiences.

I focus on clear interfaces, useful feedback, accessibility and improving products through repeated testing.
